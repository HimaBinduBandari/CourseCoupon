Our Affiliate Relationship

CourseCoupon participates in affiliate marketing programs. Some links on this website are affiliate links. This means we may earn a commission when you click on an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, at no additional cost to you.

1. What Are Affiliate Links?

Affiliate links are special tracking links that help identify when a visitor reaches a third-party website through CourseCoupon.

When you use an affiliate link to visit a course provider and complete a qualifying purchase, CourseCoupon may receive a commission from that provider.

2. How Does CourseCoupon Earn Money?

CourseCoupon shares online courses, promotional offers, coupon codes and discounted learning opportunities.

Some of the course links and coupon buttons on our website may contain affiliate tracking. If you purchase a course through those links, we may receive a commission.

These commissions help us maintain the website, update course listings and continue providing course deals to our visitors.

3. Does It Cost You Anything Extra?

No. Purchasing a course through an affiliate link does not add an extra charge to your purchase.

The price you pay and any applicable discount are determined by the course provider.

4. Our Commitment to Visitors

Our goal is to help visitors discover useful online courses and available discounts. We aim to make the course discovery process simple and transparent.

  • We aim to share relevant courses, promotional offers and coupon information.
  • Browsing CourseCoupon and accessing course information is free.
  • Affiliate commissions do not determine the price you pay.
  • We do not guarantee that every coupon will remain valid or available.
  • Course prices, discounts, availability and content may change at any time.
  • You are free to decide whether or not to purchase a course.

5. Third-Party Websites

When you click a course link or coupon button, you may be redirected to a third-party website, such as Udemy.

Any purchase you make on those websites is subject to their respective terms, conditions, payment policies, refund policies and privacy policies.

CourseCoupon does not control third-party websites or their transactions.

6. Coupon Availability and Pricing

Coupon codes, promotional offers and course prices are provided by third-party course providers and may change without notice.

Although we make efforts to keep our listings updated, we cannot guarantee that every coupon will work or that a particular discount will remain available.

Always check the final price and offer details on the course provider's website before completing a purchase.
