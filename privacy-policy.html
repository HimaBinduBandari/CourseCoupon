Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how CourseCoupon handles information when you visit and use our website.

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CourseCoupon handles information when visitors browse our website, search for courses, view coupons or follow links to third-party websites. We aim to be transparent about our practices.

1. About CourseCoupon

CourseCoupon is an online platform that helps visitors discover online courses, promotional offers, coupon codes and discounted learning opportunities.

This policy applies to visitors accessing our website and its related pages and features.

2. Information We Collect

You can browse many parts of CourseCoupon without creating an account or providing personal information.

Depending on the features and services enabled on our website, certain information may be collected, including:

  • Technical information: Browser type, device information, operating system and IP address.
  • Usage information: Pages visited, browsing activity, referral sources and interactions with website features.
  • Information you provide: Information voluntarily submitted through contact forms or communications, if those features are available.

We do not require visitors to register an account merely to browse publicly available course listings.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Operating and maintaining our website.
  • Improving website performance and user experience.
  • Understanding which courses and categories are popular.
  • Monitoring website traffic and usage patterns.
  • Responding to questions and support requests.
  • Protecting the website against misuse and security threats.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that websites may store on your device to support website functionality and remember certain preferences.

CourseCoupon may use cookies or similar technologies through its own features or third-party services, depending on the tools enabled on the website.

These technologies may be used for:

  • Basic website functionality.
  • Understanding website traffic and performance.
  • Measuring interactions with promotional links.
  • Supporting third-party affiliate tracking.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features or third-party services may not work as expected if cookies are disabled.

5. Analytics and Third-Party Services

We may use third-party analytics or website services to understand how visitors use CourseCoupon and to improve our website.

If enabled, these services may collect technical and usage information through cookies or similar technologies. Their data practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any third-party services they use.

6. Affiliate Links

CourseCoupon participates in affiliate marketing programs. Some course links and coupon buttons may contain affiliate tracking links.

When you click an affiliate link, the third-party provider may use cookies or other tracking technologies to attribute a purchase to our website.

These third parties may collect information according to their own privacy policies. CourseCoupon does not control their data collection practices.

For more information, please read our Affiliate Disclosure.

7. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external platforms, including Udemy and other course providers.

When you leave CourseCoupon and visit an external website, that website's privacy policy and terms apply.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites.

8. Data Sharing

CourseCoupon does not sell visitors' personal information as a business activity.

Information may be processed or shared where necessary with service providers operating website functionality, analytics, security or affiliate tracking services, or where required by applicable law.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law and the information involved, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise questions or concerns about our data practices.

You can also manage browser cookies through your browser settings.

11. Children's Privacy

CourseCoupon is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests relating to this Privacy Policy, please contact the CourseCoupon website team through our website's contact channel.
